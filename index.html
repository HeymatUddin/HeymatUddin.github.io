How I approach a problem

01. Understand the need

Start with the user, the symptoms and the intended outcome. Make the problem clear before choosing a tool.

02. Work through the details

Break the task down. Build or troubleshoot one part at a time, keeping access and data handling in mind.

03. Verify and document

Check the behaviour, record what changed and explain the limitations. Leave the next person something useful.
